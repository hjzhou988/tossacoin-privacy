Frequently asked questions

How do I toss the coin?

Type your two options into the fields (or leave them empty to use Heads and Tails), then flick the coin upward with your finger or tap the Toss button. The faster you flick, the higher the coin flies and the more it spins. The winning option appears when the coin lands.

Is the result really random?

Yes. Each toss has an equal chance of landing on either side.

How does the free trial work?

You can use every feature free for 7 days, starting from the day you first download the app. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks the app permanently. There is no subscription, and nothing is charged automatically when the trial ends.

I already bought the unlock. How do I get it back on a new device or after reinstalling?

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Then open the unlock screen (tap the banner at the top of the main screen) and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does the app collect my data?

No. The app does not collect any personal information or usage data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
