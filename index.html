Toss a Coin – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Toss a Coin ("the app") is a simple app that flips a virtual coin to help you make a decision. Your privacy matters, so the app is designed to work without collecting any information about you.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal information or usage data. There are no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools, and the app does not include any third-party code that collects data.

Information stored on your device

The options you type in and the toss results stay on your device and are not saved or sent anywhere. To manage the 7-day free trial, the app may store the date of its first launch on your device. This date never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

In-app purchases

The one-time "Unlock" purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or have access to your name, payment details, or Apple Account information. Apple's handling of this information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy. The app only checks with Apple whether the unlock has been purchased and when the app was first downloaded, in order to enable the purchase and the free trial.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
